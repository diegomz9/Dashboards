### Key Insights

- Overall performance of **$2.26M** in revenue from nearly 5,000 orders and a average order value of **$459**.
- Logistics performance — average delivery in under **4 days** supports strong customer satisfaction.
- Strong **seasonal pattern**: lowest sales in February, clear peaks in **November–December** (holiday/Q4 effect), with a noticeable dip mid-year.
- **Regional concentration**: West (31.4%) + East (29.6%) = **61%** of total sales. Dependency risk

### Regional Sales Breakdown

| Region    | Sales       | Share   |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| West      | $710.22K   | 31.4%   |
| East      | $669.52K   | 29.6%   |
| Central   | $492.65K   | 21.8%   |
| South     | $389.15K   | 17.2%   |

- West leads comfortably; South underperforms relative to population/market potential.
- **Recommendation**: Increase marketing spend and targeted promotions in South and Central to rebalance geographic contribution.

### Sales Performance by Month

- February is the clear low point (~$50k).
- November–December consistently the strongest months.
- Opportunity: Run mid-year promotions (May–August) to flatten the seasonal curve and boost overall annual revenue.
